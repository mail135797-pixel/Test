# 8-Image Storyboard: B099DLV8QN (Anker Soundcore Life P3)

## Overall Strategy
- **Theme**: Premium yet accessible, high-tech, functional.
- **Tone**: Smart, Reliable, Immersive.
- **Colors**: Brand Navy/Black, White, Soundcore Blue accents.

## Image 1: Main Image
- **Objective**: Compliant, clear product shot.
- **Visual**: Earbuds (both L/R) + Charging Case (slightly open or closed next to it) on pure white background (RGB 255,255,255).
- **Text**: None.
- **Compliance**: No props, no borders, no text. 85%+ frame coverage.
